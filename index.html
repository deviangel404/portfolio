A Few Of My Latest Creations

Sleep Deprived

An expressive digital illustration created in Adobe Illustrator, portraying a fast food worker caught in the chaos of burnout. With heavy eye bags, a tilted head, and mid-flip burgers, the piece captures exhaustion in motion. Set against an illustrative flame backdrop, the vibrant and dynamic color palette adds intensity, reflecting the heat and pressure of overwork.

  • Illustration
  • Adobe Illustrator
Drift Rally - Business Cards

Designed for a motorsport-focused organization, this business card set combines personalized design with brand consistency. Each card features a custom car silhouette tailored to the individual, enhancing personal identity within the team. Created in Adobe Illustrator and mockup-rendered in Photoshop, the design maintains a clean yet dynamic aesthetic with a bold black, white, and red palette. A minimalist logo on the back keeps the branding strong and impactful, while an integrated QR code offers easy access to digital contact details or portfolios.

  • Branding
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Adobe Photoshop
Moments In The Sky

A visually emotive poster blending photography and personal writing, “Moments in the Sky” captures a black-and-white scene of a couple gazing at a ferris wheel, evoking nostalgia and stillness. The bold, blue title contrasts powerfully against the grayscale backdrop, drawing immediate attention. A short original poem subtly integrates into the composition, enhancing its emotional depth. The photograph was refined using Adobe Lightroom and composed into a final layout with Photoshop for presentation.

  • Adobe Lightroom
  • Adobe Photoshop
Nishinoya - The Guardian Angel

An anime-inspired illustrative poster paying tribute to Nishinoya, the libero from Haikyuu!!, known as the team’s “Guardian Angel.” Created entirely in Adobe Illustrator, the artwork captures a dynamic mid-receive pose with the volleyball in motion, emphasizing movement and tension. Focused details like the character’s intense gaze and the bold Haikyuu!! logo bring the composition to life. Halftone textures mimic traditional manga styling, while the precise linework showcases advanced pen tool technique and control.

  • Illustration
  • Adobe Illustrator
Espresso Code - UI Design

A semi-minimalist web design created for Espresso Code, a concept art café blending creativity and coffee culture. Designed in Figma and mocked up in Photoshop, the interface showcases the café’s aesthetic through warm tones of brown, orange, and white. The layout highlights key elements such as artwork, menu features, and ambient visuals, all while maintaining an intuitive user flow. The design captures the brand’s unique vibe, offering a visually inviting and user-friendly experience.

  • UI Design
  • Figma
  • Adobe Photoshop
Lens Voyage - Magazine Design

A clean, minimal magazine layout designed for Lens Voyage, a publication centered on travel and photography. The design emphasizes a strong visual hierarchy, placing the feature photograph at the forefront with subtle typographic elements layered above. This issue spotlights renowned photographer and influencer Peter McKinnon, with carefully styled headlines and article teasers complementing the imagery. The layout focuses on clarity and aesthetic balance, aligning with the brand’s elegant and image-driven identity.

Scarlet Note

Scarlet Note is a sleek music platform concept featuring a minimal and visually striking UI. Designed in Figma and mocked up in Photoshop, the interface combines a red, white, and black color palette to evoke a bold yet refined tone. Featured artists are presented in black-and-white edits, adding contrast and focus to the overall aesthetic. The layout prioritizes ease of navigation and user engagement, balancing form and function for a seamless listening experience.

  • UI Design

  • Adobe Photoshop
Drifting By

A vibrant and humorous illustration depicting a chaotic biker mid-drift, packages flying and a red traffic light signaling too late. Created entirely in Adobe Illustrator, this piece leans into exaggerated expression and motion, with detailed pen tool work showcasing control and creativity. Small elements like a curious bird on a sign add charm, while the dynamic color palette enhances the sense of movement and storytelling. The composition blends energy, comedy, and illustrative depth in a playful yet polished execution.
